SkyPDF 3.1.2

• MyPDF is now SkyPDF (formerly MyPDF) on Microsoft Store. Your existing settings, stamps, signatures, review files and editable PDF objects remain compatible.
• The new name appears in the app, menus, About and installers. This is an update to the same Store app; no new account or migration is required.
• Fixed a Windows offline-translation shutdown hang. Repeated translation jobs now release worker threads cleanly.
• Includes offline English, Turkish and German OCR. Native x64 and ARM64 downloads are also available on GitHub; this Store package is x64.
• Continues the 3.1 feature freeze, focused on reliability and performance.

Also includes threaded note replies, voice notes, revision tools, Prepare for Sharing, touch controls, customizable Home and the visual stamp editor.
