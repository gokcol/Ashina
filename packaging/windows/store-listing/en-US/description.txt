Ashina is a native desktop PDF reader and editor for everyday document work. Read, annotate, edit, organize, fill, and sign PDFs in one focused workspace. Your documents are processed on your computer, with no account or cloud upload required.

READ COMFORTABLY
Open documents in compact tabs, drag to reorder them, and organize them into named groups such as Work or Personal. Find open files in the searchable tab list and use right-click actions to manage tabs and groups. The Windows taskbar Jump List provides compact group shortcuts, recent PDFs, and Open/New actions; choosing an open document returns to its existing tab. Navigate with thumbnails and outlines, search text, and choose the zoom and reading layout that suits your screen. Start a blank PDF whenever you need a fresh page. Use the compact File, Edit, View and other application menus to reach commands. Save updates the current file; Save As lets you choose another name. Open Recent in File and the Open hover menu lists your last 10 PDFs newest first and returns an already-open file to its existing tab and group.

MAKE IT YOUR WORKSPACE
Customize Home by reordering or hiding its Drop Area, Tools, Recents and Favorites. Choose Studio (the default), Sage, Graphite, Glacier or Calico styling and Selective accents, Color families or Soft duotone icons. Appearance choices keep the controls in their familiar positions. Slim accent sliders and precise number fields make values easier to adjust, including line and arrow head sizes; property panels keep their scrollbars clear of the controls. Signature settings are grouped together for easy management.

TOUCH YOUR DOCUMENTS
Use Automatic touchscreen detection, Touch or Mouse only mode. Toggle finger drawing, pan and pinch with two fingers, and hold to open a context menu. A compact touch icon opens settings for larger targets, long-press delay, stroke smoothing, gesture hints and a movable floating toolbar. Mouse input remains available.

EDIT AND ANNOTATE
Drag inside a selected text box to move it, use its handles to resize, or double-click to type. Choose font family and regular, bold or italic styles. Edit document text directly on the page, including supported rotated text; adjust width with automatic height and use precise rotation controls. Edit content and annotations stay separate. Choose White, light yellow, light blue or Mint text-box backgrounds, or use a custom color; adjust the text and background opacity together. Select supported existing text or images automatically in Edit mode. Highlight, underline, use squiggly underlines or strike out passages, add notes and callouts, and draw with the pen or eraser. Choose grouped shapes, lines and arrows with stroke styles, pattern fills and independent start/end heads. Use standard stamps or your own SVG and image artwork. Customize stamp text and colors, or open the visual stamp editor to arrange objects, edit fonts and shapes, apply six border templates, and adjust backgrounds and transparency. Undo/Redo and advanced SVG source editing are available. Move, resize, recolor, rotate, and flip supported objects. Save and manage reusable styles for different purposes.

REVIEW AND SHARE
Keep note conversations together with author names, dates, replies and Resolve/Reopen in the right panel, or read notes in a popover beside the marker. PDF replies use standard thread links for compatible readers. Record local voice notes from Comment, play them back and export the audio; saved PDFs include standard WAV attachments. Mark revisions with clouds, replacement suggestions and insertion notes. Export numbered PDF review summaries or CSV, and exchange Ashina review files with duplicate and conflict checks. Prepare for Sharing creates a separate copy with selected comments, metadata and attachments removed, or flattens visible pages to exclude hidden content.

WORK WITH TABLES
Create movable or embedded tables, edit cells, resize rows and columns, set colors and spacing, and apply built-in templates. Detect supported bordered tables in a selected area and detach them for editing.

SIGN AND FILL
Create a visual signature by drawing, typing, or importing an image. Add certificate-based digital signatures using a P12/PFX file or a supported Windows certificate-store identity, with sharp vector text and ink in the visible signature. Fill PDF forms, create form fields, and import or export form data.

ORGANIZE YOUR PAGES
Reorder, insert, delete, extract, merge, split, rotate, and crop pages. New blank and image-created pages default to A4. Resize individual pages without scaling their contents; inserted images fit the page proportionally. Export multiple PDF pages onto each sheet using preset or custom grids, with adjustable margins, headers, footers, and watermarks.

CONVERT AND KEEP WORK MOVING
Drag and drop files or use File → Insert Files to add PDFs, images, TXT, Markdown, SVG, DOCX and RTF to existing or new PDFs. TIFF imports all pages; animated GIF and WebP use the first frame. DOCX/RTF import requires installed Microsoft Word or LibreOffice, and Windows HEIC/HEIF import requires the HEIF codec. No document converter is downloaded.
Convert images to PDF and use the compact Export ribbon for Word, Text, Markdown and Image, with advanced options in the side panel. Export pages as PNG or JPEG. Use undo, safe saving, and local recovery copies while you work.

EVERYDAY WORKFLOWS
View and edit document metadata through File → Document Properties, with Undo/Redo and saved PDF Info/XMP synchronization.
Reuse local document templates, batch-compress PDFs or add page numbers and watermarks, and prepare separate sharing copies with selected comments, attachments and metadata removed. Match nearby text styling and align objects using optional table and baseline guides.

TRANSLATE ON YOUR COMPUTER
Translate selected text or pages locally using downloadable language packs, with no cloud fallback. Read the result in the side panel or attach it as a note. Model downloads require internet access; document text stays on your computer.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
Ashina has no advertising, usage analytics, or automatic document uploads. Settings, custom stamps, signatures, and recovery data stay in local application storage. You choose where to save and share your files.

Text editing depends on the PDF's structure and fonts. Markdown and Word exports focus on text and may not preserve complex layouts. DOCX/RTF imports use the installed office application to preserve layout. Certificate trust depends on the recipient's PDF viewer and trust settings. Recognize text on selected scanned pages with offline OCR in English, Turkish and German. Review the completion summary, then search or select recognized text.
