Ashina - a PDF editor for reading, annotating, editing, filling forms,
comparing versions and signing.

Ashina was built with the help of Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, to make
everyday business paperwork easier. It is provided as is, without warranty
of any kind: keep copies of important documents and use it at your own risk.

Privacy: Ashina works on this computer. It does not send your documents or
any other information to other computers unless you ask it to, for example
by opening a web link in a document.

What the installer changes:
- It copies Ashina to the folder you choose and adds it to the Start menu.
- If you tick the option, it adds Ashina to the "Open with" menu for PDF
  files. Your default PDF app stays the same.
- You can remove Ashina at any time in Settings > Apps; that also removes
  the "Open with" entry. Your saved signatures and details are kept in
  your user profile.
